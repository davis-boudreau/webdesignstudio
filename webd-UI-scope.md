Across those examples, beginners should also learn:

- Links and buttons: links navigate; buttons perform actions.
- Form grouping: fieldset and legend for related checkboxes or radio buttons.
- Accessibility: skip link, visible focus, meaningful labels and logical reading order.
- Responsive foundations: normal flow first, then Flexbox and Grid; flexible images, sensible breakpoints and no page overflow.
- Visual consistency: reusable colours, spacing, borders and typography.
  I’d keep badges, notices and a simple data table as optional additions. Tabs, modals and carousels can wait until a later course.

One distinction to teach carefully: a card can use article when its content stands independently; aside is for related information, regardless of where CSS positions it.
